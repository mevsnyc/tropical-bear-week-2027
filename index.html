Tropical by nature.
Bear by tradition.

South Florida's original bear gathering, rebuilt from the sand up for 2027 with a fresh Miami deco soul.

Every March, thousands of bears, cubs, otters and admirers wash into Fort Lauderdale for a week of pool parties, beach days, boat cruises and late-night dancefloors. It's loud, it's hairy, it's gloriously tropical — and it's the warmest welcome in the bear world. 2027 turns the whole thing up: more venues, more parties, more sun.
